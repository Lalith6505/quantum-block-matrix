Privacy Policy

App: Quantum Block Matrix (com.quantum.blockmatrix) Developer: Lalith Madanayake Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction & Philosophy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Quantum Block Matrix, developed and maintained by Lalith Madanayake. We are staunch advocates of digital privacy. This policy provides complete transparency regarding how our software operates.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Quantum Block Matrix does NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or monetize any personal information from its users:

  • No collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No device telemetry (No IMEI, no Android Advertising ID, no IDFA, no IP logging).
  • No geolocation or Wi-Fi network detection.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporting SDKs (Zero Firebase, Facebook, or Google Analytics SDKs).

3. 100% Ad-Free Guarantee

Quantum Block Matrix is a paid, premium game priced at a one-time purchase of USD $1.99 / $2.00. It contains ZERO advertisements. You will never experience banner ads, pop-ups, interstitial commercials, or rewarded videos.

4. Hardware Permissions

The application requests a single hardware permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to generate tactile haptic feedback when dragging and placing blocks, or clearing multi-line combos. Haptics can be toggled off at any time in the in-game settings.

5. Local Device Storage

Your high scores, campaign quest stars (levels 1 through 50), and audio toggles are saved exclusively in your device's local private sandbox storage. This data never leaves your device.

6. Children’s Privacy

Quantum Block Matrix is suitable for all ages (PEGI 3, ESRB Everyone). Because we do not collect any personal data, we fully comply with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policy.
