Privacy Policy

Application: Quantum Block Matrix (com.quantum.blockmatrix) Version: 1.0.0 Effective Date: October 14, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy-First Philosophy

Quantum Block Matrix is engineered and published by independent developer Lalith Madanayake. We firmly believe that mobile gaming should be an enjoyable, serene sanctuary free from data harvesting, surveillance capitalism, and advertising trackers.

This Privacy Policy explains in transparent, plain language our strict policy of zero data collection and describes how your privacy is completely respected across Android, iOS, and Web platforms.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Quantum Block Matrix does NOT collect, harvest, record, share, or sell any personal data whatsoever:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, ages, or physical addresses within the app.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not access or store IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IDFA, IP addresses, or MAC addresses.
  • No Location Data: The application does not use GPS, Wi-Fi tri-angulation, or IP-based geolocation.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: There are zero third-party telemetry, crash-reporting, or analytics SDKs (e.g., no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK).

3. 100% Ad-Free Experience

Quantum Block Matrix is a paid, premium application priced at a one-time fee of USD $1.99 / $2.00.

The application contains ZERO advertisements. You will never encounter interstitial video ads, banner ads, native sponsored content, or rewarded video ad networks. As a result, no advertising identifier or behavioral profiling occurs.

4. Device Permissions Disclosures

Quantum Block Matrix requests the absolute minimum permissions strictly required for offline game functionality:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to actuate the device’s vibration motor for crisp tactile feedback (micro-ticks on piece drag, mechanical snap on block placement, and multi-pulse rumble on multi-line clears). You can toggle haptics on or off at any time in the in-game settings.

The application does NOT request camera, microphone, storage, contacts, location, or network permissions.

5. Local Sandbox Storage

All game state data — including high scores, 50 campaign level completion stars, and audio/haptic preferences — is stored strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences or Web LocalStorage.

This data remains completely sandboxed within your device and is never transmitted over the internet to external servers.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Quantum Block Matrix is family-friendly and rated for Everyone (PEGI 3, ESRB Everyone). Because we collect zero personal data from any user, we strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy.
